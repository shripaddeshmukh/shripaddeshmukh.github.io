<p>
    I am a fourth year PhD candidate at University of Massachusetts, Amherst, working on reinforcement learning (RL). I have the fortune to work under <a href="https://people.cs.umass.edu/~sniekum/">Prof. Scott Niekum</a> and to closely collaborate with <a href="https://people.cs.umass.edu/~pthomas/"> Prof. Philip Thomas</a>. My research focuses on designing algorithms for safe and efficient reinforcement learning (RL).
</p>
<p>
    Earlier, I spent time at Adobe (India) tackling novel machine learning challenges (2020-2023). Prior to that, I completed a BTech in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras (2016-2020).
</p>
<p>
    In my leisure time, I like to hang out with my friends, read books, write "something", and explore places.
